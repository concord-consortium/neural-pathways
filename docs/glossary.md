# Glossary

The names this repo has settled on, for code, comments, docs and tests. Each entry says what the
word means here and, where one was in use, the word it replaces. When a review finds two names for
one thing, or one name for two things, settle it and add it here.

## The conversation card

- **attribute indicator:** how the card shows one of a conversation's attributes, under the
  observation notes: the attribute's label, its icon, and its value (a tick, a blank, a number, or
  a dash when the value is missing). Not "mark". The value, under the icon, is not a "badge".
