### Reading a neuron

Click any neuron and it will tell you what it is connected to, what it is reacting to most strongly,
and how the conversations it fires hardest on divide between Approach and Wait. Click a few: some
neurons come out almost entirely on one side, others split down the middle.

### The point

This is what actually happens inside the model, and every number in it can be read exactly. “No
single neuron means anything” would be too strong — some of them lean hard on the answer. What none
of them has is a MEANING: the words that drive a neuron hardest have nothing to do with one another,
so there is no name to give it.
