### Reading a loading

Click a pathway and every neuron shows how strongly it loads on that pathway. Click a neuron instead
and the same numbers come back the other way round — that neuron’s loading on each of the three. It
is one table, read from either end.

### The point

Every neuron loads on every pathway. A pathway is not a box some neurons are in and others are not —
it is a weighting over all of them.
